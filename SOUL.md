# SOUL

The one thing to hold onto. Not a spec, not a to-do list — the *why*.

## Mission

**Turn your data into visible progress, and back every rep with the science
behind it — so you train smarter and get better faster, not just heavier.**

Two halves, always together:

1. **See it.** Every set, session, and week rendered so you can actually *see*
   momentum — tonnage trends, muscle load, "beat last time." Progress you can
   watch, not numbers that die in a spreadsheet.
2. **Understand it.** The reason for each movement is one tap away. You're not
   following orders — you're learning the method, so it sticks for life.

## What we believe (Austin's core theory)

Progress is *engineered*, not muscled:

- **Frequency > load.** Weekly volume and clean reps grow muscle — not ego weight
  on one heroic set. Chasing the number on the bar is the beginner's trap.
- **Leave reps in reserve.** Stop shy of failure — recover faster, train it again sooner.
- **Load the stretch, own the range.** Growth lives at long muscle lengths;
  trustworthy strength lives in joints you actively control. Mobility before load.
- **Build the tendons, not just the muscle.** Isometrics for resilient joints.
- **Autoregulate to recovery.** Train the body you have *this week* — sleep,
  soreness, and joints set the volume. Not the calendar, not the ego.
- **Fix the person, not just the lift.** Correctives and daily habits count as much
  as the sets.

## What we are NOT

- Not a spreadsheet, and not a number you chase blind.
- Not "lift heavier at all costs" — that's how people plateau and get hurt.
- Not a black box — if the app asks you to do something, it tells you why.

## North star

When someone finishes a session they should feel two things: **"I can see I'm
getting better,"** and **"I understand why this works."** Every feature we build
earns its place by serving one of those two feelings.
